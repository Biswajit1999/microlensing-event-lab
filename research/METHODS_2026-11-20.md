Scientific scope: uniform finite-exposure PSPL midpoint integration, quadrature convergence and fixed-impact blend degeneracy.

The microlensing integrator assumes uniform exposure response, a point source and point lens; it does not model finite-source effects, seeing or the OGLE shutter response. A blend family at fixed impact parameter is not a lens-mass measurement.
